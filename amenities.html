( Amenities )

Everything you need, nothing you don’t.

Free WiFi

Complimentary WiFi throughout the hotel and in every room, at no extra cost.

Private Parking

On-site parking available from £15/day, valid from 2pm on arrival to 11am on departure. Please contact us ahead to reserve a space, as spots are limited.

Family Rooms

Spacious family rooms sleeping up to four guests — two single beds and one double, ideal for families or small groups.

Heating

Every room is heated for year-round comfort, whatever the London weather is doing.

Daily Housekeeping

Rooms are serviced daily. Bed linen and towels are refreshed on the morning of every fifth day of your stay — just leave used towels on the floor.

Luggage Storage

Drop off or store your bags on arrival or on your day of departure — departure-day storage is same-day only.

Breakfast Nearby

Full English or Italian breakfast is served 8am–11:30am at Little Napoli, a five-minute walk away. Ask reception for a voucher, and enjoy 15% off all other meals there too.

Smoke-Free Hotel

We’re a smoke-free property. A designated outdoor smoking area is available away from the entrance.

Ensuite Bathrooms

Every room has its own ensuite bathroom with shower, towels and toiletries provided.

Flat-Screen & Satellite TV

Satellite channels on a flat-screen TV in every room.

Tea & Coffee

An electric kettle and tea/coffee making facilities in every room, plus a shared guest microwave.
