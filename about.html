About Hotel 261

01 Who We Are

Simple, comfortable, and run by people who actually live here — that’s the whole idea behind Hotel 261.

Tucked into a quiet stretch of Shepherd’s Bush, Hotel 261 has been welcoming guests to West London with large rooms, on-site parking and genuinely friendly service. Every room comes with satellite TV, tea & coffee making facilities and a fridge, with a shared guest microwave available too.

We’re a short walk from Westfield London shopping centre, a handful of West London bars and cafés, and well placed for Wembley, the Hammersmith Apollo and Queens Park Rangers’ football stadium — just over 10 minutes on foot. Shepherd’s Bush Market Underground station (Circle & Hammersmith & City lines) is a 15 minute walk away.

We’re a family-run 2-star hotel, popular with solo travellers and short, one-night stays — simple and comfortable rather than flashy, at a price that makes sense for West London.

02 What Guests Get

Full English or Italian breakfast is available a five-minute walk away at Little Napoli, with vouchers arranged through reception.

On-site parking is available for £15/day, subject to availability — handy for a hotel this close to central London.

Daily housekeeping keeps rooms fresh throughout your stay, and we’ll happily store luggage on your day of departure.

Ensuite bathrooms in every room, along with a flat-screen TV, satellite channels and free WiFi throughout the building.

Family rooms sleep up to five guests — useful for visiting family or small groups who want to stay together.

We’re a smoke-free hotel with a designated outdoor smoking area, and quiet enough that solo travellers consistently rate us highly for comfort.
