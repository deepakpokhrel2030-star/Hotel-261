02 Good to Know

House rules

Check-in from 14:00–23:30. Check-out 08:00–11:00. Please arrive by 23:30 as the hotel is closed overnight. Early check-in cannot be guaranteed, but if your room is ready we’ll accommodate you.

A government-issued photo ID is required at check-in (digital or scanned IDs are not accepted). A £20 cash or ID deposit is required for room keys.

Full English or Italian breakfast served 08:00–11:30 at Little Napoli, a 5-minute walk away. Ask reception for a voucher — and enjoy 15% off all other meals there too.

Limited on-site parking, £15/day from 14:00 on arrival to 11:00 on departure. Contact us ahead to reserve a space, as availability is limited.

Cash accepted on site. Online bookings can be paid in advance or on arrival, depending on the rate you choose.

We are a smoke-free hotel with a designated outdoor smoking area. Pets are not permitted.

Luggage can be stored on arrival or departure day (departure storage is same-day only). Bicycles are not permitted inside the hotel; they may be chained outside by the car park at your own risk.
