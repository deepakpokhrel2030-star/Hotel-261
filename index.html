01 About the Hotel

Simple, comfortable, and run by people who actually live here — that’s the whole idea behind Hotel 261.

Tucked into a quiet stretch of Shepherd’s Bush, we’ve been welcoming guests to West London with large rooms, on-site parking and genuinely friendly service. Every room comes with satellite TV, tea & coffee facilities and a fridge.

We’re a short walk from Westfield London, a handful of local bars and cafés, and well placed for Wembley, the Hammersmith Apollo and Queens Park Rangers’ stadium.

07 Good to Know

House rules

Check-in from 14:00–23:30. Check-out 08:00–11:00. Please arrive by 23:30 as the hotel is closed overnight.

A government-issued photo ID is required at check-in. A £20 cash or ID deposit is required for room keys.

Full English or Italian breakfast served 08:00–11:30 at Little Napoli, a 5-minute walk away. Ask reception for a voucher.

Limited on-site parking, £15/day from 14:00 on arrival to 11:00 on departure. Contact us ahead to reserve a space.

Cash accepted on site. Online bookings can be paid in advance or on arrival, depending on rate.

We are a smoke-free hotel with a designated outdoor smoking area. Pets are not permitted.
